## Unicode emoji and character data

`Sources/LodestarCore/Resources/emoji.json` (used by **Search Emoji & Symbols**)
is generated by `scripts/generate-emoji-data.py` from these Unicode Consortium
files:

- `emoji-test.txt`, Unicode Emoji 17.0: the emoji set, short names, groups,
  order and emoji versions.
  <https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/emoji/emoji-test.txt>
- `UnicodeData.txt`, Unicode 17.0: character names for symbols.
  <https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/UnicodeData.txt>
- CLDR release 48, `common/annotations/en.xml` and
  `common/annotationsDerived/en.xml`: English names and search keywords.
  <https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr>

The selection of symbols (arrows, math, currency, punctuation, keyboard
symbols, Greek letters, shapes, box drawing) and the keyboard symbol names are
Lodestar's own. No emoji images are bundled: glyphs are drawn by the system
font (Apple Color Emoji).
