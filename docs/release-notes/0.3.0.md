## What's new in 0.3.0

- **Currency conversion as you type.** Type `100 usd to eur`, `1,250 gbp in inr`, `$20` or `50€` and the converted amount appears in the results. Return copies it, ⌘Return pastes it. Without a target currency, it converts to your Mac's regional currency. Exchange rates come from open.er-api.com and refresh at most every 6 hours.
- **Typing Test.** A new command that measures your words per minute and accuracy on random common words, shows correct and wrong letters as you type, and marks your mistakes on the results screen. Press Return for a new test.
